regions-dashboard · staging

The 2-tab Regions element: a SELECT VIEW switcher over "Regions List" and "Region Dashboards". Clicking a region or country on tab 1 swaps to tab 2 with that selection applied. Each tab is also mountable on its own, below.

profile-config.profile = "regions-mfe"  — the composite (both tabs)
profile-config.profile = "regions-list"  — tab 1 alone
profile-config.profile = "region-dashboards"  — tab 2 alone
